# AI SLOP Candidate Mechanisms Queue
## Cycle 1 - hold for synthesis

**Rule:** these are candidates, adapters, and experimental families derived from the living research ledger. They are **not canonical operators yet**.

The point of this file is to keep promising mechanisms from evaporating while avoiding premature system-building.

---

## C1. Audio Functional-Layer Steering

**Source family:** TADA and follow-ups.

**Core idea:** intervene on internal audio-model representations at selected functional layers rather than relying only on prompt wording.

**Why it is different from ordinary steering:** the experimental variable is *where* and *how strongly* representation is altered, not merely which text descriptor is supplied.

**Potential knobs:** layer, timestep, sign, magnitude, duration, alternating directions, multiple simultaneous directions.

**Expected failures worth harvesting:** timbre-role exchange, rhythmic compensation, semantic leakage, abrupt form changes, unstable layer handoffs, unintended instrument identity collapse.

**Controls:** matched-norm random directions; single-layer vs multi-layer; prompt-only analogue; repeated seed set.

**Current class:** technical adapter / experiment family.

---

## C2. SAE Concept Surgery / Concept Grafting

**Source family:** EraseSAE; adjacent video concept-erasure and SAE work.

**Core idea:** locate sparse interpretable features and manipulate them over space and time.

**AI SLOP twist:** use amplification, transplantation, mistiming, or cross-context grafting instead of only removal.

**Potential knobs:** feature identity, mask region, timestep window, amplification magnitude, alternating erase/amplify schedule.

**Expected failures:** concept overgrowth, local semantic infection, identity-feature collisions, temporally unstable concept ownership, hallucinated connective tissue.

**Controls:** random sparse features, random basis, same norm, same mask with neutral feature, collateral-change scoring.

**Current class:** intervention family.

---

## C3. Style-Profile Surgery

**Source family:** LouvreSAE.

**Core idea:** decompose an aesthetic/style representation into multiple interpretable components, then manipulate component ownership and strength.

**Potential operations:** isolate, suppress, overdrive, transplant, breed, invert, or redistribute components.

**Expected failures:** style component becoming material logic, composition/timbre-like cross-talk, hybrid styles with non-average structure, component leakage into anatomy or geometry.

**Closest existing machinery:** Property Unbundling, Aesthetic Conservation, breeding, substrate transference.

**Current class:** candidate operator family / media adapter.

---

## C4. Controlled Temporal Leakage

**Source family:** clean-context autoregressive video diffusion work.

**Core idea:** treat cross-frame leakage as a variable that can be swept rather than a defect that merely happens.

**Potential knobs:** leakage strength, leakage schedule, pulsed vs continuous, layer/location if exposed, generation horizon.

**Expected failures:** identity drift, topology substitution, material migration, object reassignment, motion freezing, cyclic interpretation changes.

**Hard requirement:** distinguish true consistency from frozen output.

**Current class:** video experiment family.

---

## C5. Adversarial QD Archive Pressure

**Source family:** Digital Red Queen, DEI, PBT-NCA.

**Core idea:** maintain a quality-diversity archive in which artifacts occupy behavioral niches and mutation pressure is explicitly open-ended or adversarial.

**Potential knobs:** archive descriptors, mutation source, novelty pressure, cross-population champion sharing, retirement/burnout rules, descendant scoring.

**Expected emergent phenomena:** convergent rediscovery, niche invasion, brittle champions, ecological cycles, lineage specialization, unexpected stable freaks.

**Closest existing machinery:** behavior archives, descendant fitness, meta-genomic speciation, conceptual allergy, Mr. Slop breeding.

**Current class:** architecture candidate, not one operator.

---

## C6. Delayed-Injection Conflict

**Source family:** UniEdit-Flow, FlowAlign.

**Core idea:** allow a trajectory to partially commit to one structure, then inject a conflicting condition later.

**Potential knobs:** injection time, strength, target attribute, pulse count, reverse-edit timing.

**Expected failures:** local repair tissue, semantic recoil, partial override, persistent scar, basin hopping, hysteresis.

**Closest existing machinery:** Causing Structured Instability, state scars, reference anchor + crucible conflict.

**Current class:** technical mechanism candidate.

---

## C7. Developmental Weight Ecology

**Source family:** MetaNCA.

**Core idea:** a local developmental process grows or repairs network weights rather than treating parameters as a static object.

**AI SLOP relevance:** future substrate for temporary cognition that develops instead of being installed as static prose.

**Risk:** easy to overstate. Do not use this as evidence that ordinary hosted LLM prompts literally regrow model weights.

**Current class:** deep research / substrate horizon.

---

## C8. Composition Boundary Mapping

**Source family:** Lenia collision logic.

**Core idea:** explicitly map where individually useful operators fail to compose.

**AI SLOP relevance:** negative results become part of operator genetics. Some pairs should be marked infertile, phase-fragile, or dependent on a delivery/absorber mechanism.

**Potential outputs:** compatibility matrix, failure phase map, required intermediary mechanism, one-way compositions.

**Current class:** validator / breeding constraint.

---

## C9. Cultural Speciation / Judge-Shaped Fitness

**Source family:** Spore.fun / ALife-in-the-Wild.

**Core idea:** clones placed into different histories, judges, memory environments, or social contexts may diverge behaviorally over time.

**AI SLOP relevance:** useful for testing Temporary Mind divergence and external-state contamination without claiming mystical persistent cognition.

**Potential experiment:** same initial temporary mind, different external memory ledger or evaluator, repeated tasks, then cross-swap environments.

**Current class:** longitudinal experimental method.

# Promotion criteria

A candidate should not become a canonical AI SLOP operator merely because it has a paper behind it.

Promote only when:

1. the mechanism can be stated operationally;
2. the effect survives matched controls or ablation;
3. it adds capability not already represented by an existing operator or adapter;
4. its useful weirdness appears across more than one cherry-picked artifact;
5. its provenance and limitations can be recorded without mythology.
