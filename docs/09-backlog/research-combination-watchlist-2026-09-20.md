# AI SLOP Research Combination Watchlist
## Do not build these yet - watch for convergence across future cycles

This file exists because several Cycle 1 findings look more interesting **together** than alone. The correct move right now is to preserve the pairings, not prematurely turn them into systems.

---

## Pairing A - TADA + existing audio trajectory control

**Ingredients:** functional-layer steering + MusicLayout / LatCH-style explicit temporal curves + AI SLOP role-exchange structures.

**Possible future synthesis:** a track has an external temporal score while internal layer steering is independently modulated over time. Instead of "make intensity rise," the system could specify *which internal functional representation* is allowed to dominate at each phase.

**Why wait:** we need to know which open audio steering implementations are actually runnable and what musical dimensions they affect reliably.

---

## Pairing B - EraseSAE + LouvreSAE

**Ingredients:** sparse feature surgery + decomposable style profiles.

**Possible future synthesis:** treat style as a set of sparse organs that can be removed, amplified, transplanted, or locally grafted into video concepts.

**Weird possibility worth preserving:** a style component ceases to behave like "style" when overexpressed and becomes geometry, material, motion, or identity pressure.

**Why wait:** both interpretability and transfer claims need random-basis and collateral-damage controls.

---

## Pairing C - Controlled temporal leakage + delayed injection

**Ingredients:** leak/drift control + late trajectory intervention.

**Possible future synthesis:** establish a stable subject, inject an incompatible rule late, then deliberately alter cross-frame leakage to decide whether the conflict remains local, spreads, oscillates, or becomes a persistent scar.

**This may become:** a genuine temporal structured-instability engine for video.

**Why wait:** transfer across model backbones is an open question.

---

## Pairing D - Digital Red Queen + PBT-NCA + Mr. Slop breeding

**Ingredients:** adversarial MAP-Elites pressure + population-based open-ended discovery + deterministic two-parent operator breeding.

**Possible future synthesis:** an archive of bred operators or artifacts competes across behavioral niches, while mutation pressure changes over generations and champions can migrate between populations.

**Critical rule:** do not collapse this into one scalar "best" score. Preserve diversity and ancestry.

**Why wait:** more research cycles may surface better archive descriptors, novelty metrics, or lineage controls.

---

## Pairing E - MetaNCA + Temporary Minds

**Ingredients:** developmental weight generation + temporary cognitive operators.

**Possible future synthesis:** Temporary Minds that are generated or repaired by a developmental rule rather than copied from a fixed prompt library.

**Important epistemic boundary:** this would require an actual controllable model/substrate. A hosted-chat prompt cannot honestly be described as growing weights.

**Why wait:** this is substrate-level work and should not be confused with prompt procedure.

---

## Pairing F - Spore.fun + external-state controllers + scar experiments

**Ingredients:** clone divergence, judge-shaped fitness, memory poisoning + AI SLOP external state + silent-scar methodology.

**Possible future synthesis:** identical agents are placed into different histories and evaluative ecologies; later, external memory is removed or swapped to test which differences persist behaviorally.

**Why wait:** we need careful definitions of state, memory, environment, and persistence to avoid anthropomorphic bullshit.

---

## Pairing G - Lenia composition boundaries + operator genetics

**Ingredients:** phase-fragile glider collisions + AI SLOP structural ancestry / breeding rules.

**Possible future synthesis:** an operator breeding system that stores not only compatible parents but also **infertility, one-way compatibility, phase windows, and required mediator operators**.

**Why wait:** more negative-results literature may give better ways to represent non-composability.

---

# Cross-cycle signals to watch for

A combination becomes synthesis-worthy when at least two independent cycles point toward the same deeper pattern, for example:

- intervention timing matters more than intervention wording;
- behavior archives outperform appearance-only archives;
- persistent effects depend on external state or trajectory history;
- decomposed features are more useful than monolithic concepts;
- diversity pressure produces better weirdness than a single optimization target;
- failures become controllable when the mechanism is isolated;
- useful combinations require explicit compatibility constraints rather than "stack more weird stuff."

When several of those recur, *then* build the monster.
