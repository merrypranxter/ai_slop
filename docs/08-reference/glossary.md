# Glossary — Project Language Without the Mysticism Leak

This file defines recurring AI SLOP terms at the level the canonical repo actually means them. Historical sources sometimes use the same words more dramatically.

## AI slop
In this project: interesting generative residue created when a system has to negotiate difficult or incompatible constraints — not simply low-quality AI output.

## Structured instability
A deliberately constructed condition in which several pressures remain active, their ordinary resolutions are incompatible, and enough scaffolding remains for the system to invent a structured compromise rather than collapse into arbitrary noise.

## Caused weirdness
A strange result traceable to an operation, conflict, developmental history, or controlled perturbation. Opposite of “just ask for weird.”

## Operator
A rule that changes what transitions, outputs, or solutions are legal/likely/selected. Examples: transfer a property, delete a primitive, change a distance metric, hold an invariant while another subsystem accelerates.

## Mechanism
The operation plus a causal account for why it should alter the system. A mechanism claim can be PROCEDURAL, SUPPORTED, HYPOTHESIS, SPECULATIVE, etc. Do not promote a cool operator name into a proven mechanism automatically.

## Invariant / anchor
A property intentionally held stable or recognizable while other dimensions mutate. Anchors prevent total dissolution and create pressure by making change occur around something that refuses to move.

## Scar
A path-dependent change that persists after the condition that caused it is gone. A scar can be semantic, structural, musical, visual, symbolic, or controller-state based.

## Fossil / fossilization
An accidental feature, error, compression artifact, symbol, or intermediate structure that becomes persistent because later generations reuse it as conditioning/state. Fossilization is observable when a later state still carries an artifact after the original cause is removed.

## State
The current explicit representation owned by software or an experimental procedure. It may include semantic features, structural traits, medium-specific variables, invariants, scars, uncertainty, mappings, and history pointers. It is not synonymous with a model’s hidden activations.

## Trajectory / route
The ordered series of transformations taken by a state. In path-dependent systems, the route is part of the artifact.

## Path dependence
The claim/procedure that `A → C` may differ from `A → B → C` because passing through B changes what arrives at C. In software this can be enforced explicitly; in native model behavior it should be tested rather than assumed.

## Ledger
Persistent record of state changes: mutations, symbols, route events, scars, attractors, or diagnostics.

## Attractor
A recurring/stable regime in an iterative system. In TOPOS/SRE this is an externally measured pattern or controller concept, not a claim that we have identified a literal hidden neural attractor without instrumentation.

## Perturbation
A deliberate change introduced to test, escape, or reorganize a regime. Good perturbations alter one interpretable variable when possible.

## Regime shift
A qualitative change in the behavior of an iterative system: different structure, semantics, symbol use, stability, or transition pattern.

## Useful lie
Project term for language that is experientially accurate but technically simplified. The major example is “navigating latent space” in the Semantic Manifold Game. The software actually manipulates an external operational analogue built from representations and explicit state.

## Latent space
Use carefully. Conversationally: the project’s intuitive name for conceptual neighborhoods and strange routes through generator possibilities. Technically: specify the actual representation being manipulated — embedding vectors, image latents, audio features, hidden states, explicit controller state, etc. There is no single universal master latent map.

## Semantic manifold
In the game architecture: an externally constructed navigable geometry combining embeddings, explicit traits, relations, history, metrics, transformations, and model-generated decompositions. Not a direct view into proprietary hidden states.

## Concept transduction
Turning a concept from a noun/theme into operations that can affect a target medium. Example: thin-film interference becomes reinforcement/cancellation under small phase differences rather than merely “iridescent.”

## Jurisdiction
A subsystem’s exclusive responsibility. In Suno work, harmony, melody, rhythm, timbre, performance, and anchor can be given separate rule systems so they negotiate rather than blend into one mushy genre average.

## Productive contradiction under constraint
Audio/general pattern: assign incompatible systems separate jurisdictions while preserving an invariant and then apply transformations that force negotiation. Contradiction is productive only while both sides remain active.

## Semantic recoil
A later state changes the active interpretation of an earlier state; subsequent reasoning uses the revised earlier meaning rather than restoring the pristine original.

## Recall mutation
A remembered concept is reconstructed under the current context; that scarred reconstruction becomes the next remembered version.

## Temporary Mind
A procedural installation that changes how candidates are generated, compared, selected, preserved, or transformed for a task/session. It is not a literal temporary rewrite of neural weights or a claim of new consciousness.

## Synthetic transducer
A procedure that makes an abstract variable detectable through an invented measurement/sensation and lets that signal affect later selection.

## Synthetic valence
A procedure that assigns an invented attraction/aversion/protection response to a detectable trigger, changing what gets attention or preservation.

## Generative desire / alien utility
A candidate-selection objective different from ordinary “best answer” criteria while user requirements remain hard constraints.

## Primitive deletion
Remove a foundational assumption with **no replacement**, then reconstruct around the wound.

## Ontological recompilation
Replace a foundational primitive with a different one and rederive downstream concepts that genuinely depend on it.

## Axiom fracture / negative load-bearing synthesis
Negate a hidden assumption while preserving the original goal, then require the negation itself to become the mechanism that achieves the goal.

## Omission field
The target remains real but cannot be represented directly; infer/generate it from multiple independent external consequences.

## Forced aliasing
Deliberately discard dimensions until unrelated concepts become indistinguishable, then reconstruct without restoring the missing information.

## Artifact family
A recurring class of outputs produced across seeds/inputs under the same manipulation. More useful experimentally than one favorite freak result.

## Creative utility
How artistically fertile, surprising, usable, or delightful an experiment is.

## Mechanism confidence
How strongly evidence supports the causal explanation for the result. Creative utility and mechanism confidence must be scored separately.

## Ablation
Remove one supposedly causal ingredient while holding the rest as constant as possible. If the characteristic effect remains, the story needs revision.

## Dose-response sweep
Vary one intervention continuously/in ordered levels rather than comparing only OFF and MAXIMUM.

## Condition dropout
The system resolves a contradiction by ignoring one condition rather than producing a meaningful compromise.

## Mechanism theater
A prompt/document narrates sophisticated internal machinery, but the actual procedure does not force a different selection or state transition.

## Persona theater
A voice/character sounds strange while the underlying idea-generation procedure remains ordinary.

## No free weirdness
Project law: weirdness should be downstream of an identifiable rule, conflict, history, detector, constraint, or transformation. Randomness can be material, but randomness alone is not the mechanism.

## Compile / compiler layer
Canonical architecture treats prompts as a projection of deeper state/operator logic into the format a target generator accepts. The prompt is output from the system, not necessarily the project’s primary artifact.
