# Lens Palette — Thinkers, Sciences, and Other Ways to Bend the Problem

This file distills the two large source palettes into a usable **creative-lens reference**. These are not extra project mechanisms by themselves. A lens earns its keep only when it changes what is attended to, what relations are modeled, or what operations are proposed.

Primary sources:

- `ai_readable/art/06-weird-thinkers-persona-palette.md`
- `ai_readable/art/07-weird-sciences-persona-palette.md`
- supporting examples in `ai_readable/art/05-weird-promptness-roundtable.md`

## How to use a lens

Do not ask “what would this person/science make it look like?” Ask:

1. What does this lens make visible that the default framing ignores?
2. What relation, process, boundary, scale, or failure does it privilege?
3. What **operator** follows from that change?
4. Can the operator be stated without the proper noun?
5. If the name is removed and nothing changes, the lens was only costume.

A good sequence is:

`problem → lens → overlooked relation → operator → medium transduction → test`

## Thinker / theory lenses

### Apparatus and mediation
Use when the recording/display system should become part of the causal problem rather than a transparent window.

Source palette includes Vilém Flusser and related media/apparatus thinking. Productive questions:

- What does the apparatus permit, forbid, or normalize?
- What happens when the medium becomes an actor?
- Which artifact belongs to the capture system rather than the represented subject?

Canonical destinations: **Observer / Medium Coupling**, **Causal Artifact Chain**, GLSL/state visualization.

### Cut-up, recombination, and ownership failure
The William Burroughs-style cut-up lens is useful when syntax, continuity, or property ownership should be severed and recombined under rules rather than random collage.

Operational descendants include:

- property unbundling;
- destructive compression;
- forced aliasing;
- symbolic reassignment;
- controlled splice / sequence discontinuity.

### Process ontology
Deleuze/Guattari-like process emphasis and related process philosophies are useful when nouns have become too stable.

Ask what rates, flows, gradients, couplings, maintenance processes, and transitions keep the visible object temporarily assembled.

Canonical descendant: **Kinetic Dissolution / Metabolic Vector Reorganization**.

### Umwelt / perspectival worlds
A Jakob von Uexküll-style Umwelt lens asks what the system can detect and therefore what world effectively exists for it.

Canonical descendants:

- Synthetic Transducer;
- Synthetic Valence;
- alien utility / salience systems.

This is stronger than “imagine an alien perspective” because it requires detectors, signals, blind spots, and downstream action.

### Hyperobject / scale mismatch
Timothy Morton-style hyperobject thinking is useful when a phenomenon cannot be localized cleanly in one frame, scale, or causal neighborhood.

Operational questions:

- What is locally visible but globally distributed?
- Which scales disagree?
- What traces remain when the whole object cannot appear at once?

Useful for video, route systems, distributed audio form, and large-scale semantic-state experiments.

### Cyborg / boundary instability
Donna Haraway-like boundary trouble is useful where human/machine, organism/environment, natural/artificial, or subject/instrument ownership should be made operationally ambiguous.

Do not stop at cyborg imagery. Change who owns a property, where a boundary is enforced, or which subsystem performs which function.

### Surrealist / oneiric logic
Surrealist, Artaud, Bataille, Philip K. Dick, and related entries in the source palette are strongest when used to alter **identity rules, causal order, reality commitment, or category membership** rather than to request dreamy prose.

Canonical descendants include Oneiric Contamination, Error Axiomatization, Omission Field, and Semantic Recoil.

### Constraint art and anti-cliché systems
Use formal constraint traditions when ordinary creativity keeps converging on the same attractive solution. The important move is not “be experimental”; it is to define legal moves, forbidden moves, exhaustion, substitution, or rule turnover.

Canonical descendants include Procedural Taboo, Structural Satiation, Cliché Mortality Tax, and Operator Burnout.

## Scientific / biological lenses

### Reaction–diffusion and morphogenesis
**Grounding:** well-established mathematical/biological pattern-forming framework; any mapping into image/audio remains a creative transduction rather than evidence that a generator literally runs a reaction–diffusion solver.

Useful operations:

- local activation + inhibition;
- spreading fronts;
- competing growth fields;
- pattern bifurcation;
- threshold-dependent state change.

Canonical destinations: image morphogenesis, audio motif growth, cross-domain simulation.

### Slime mold / Physarum-style distributed problem solving
**Grounding:** real biological behavior is a valid inspiration; claims about “intelligence” should be phrased at the level supported by the specific example.

Useful relations:

- distributed exploration;
- reinforcement of efficient paths;
- retraction from low-value paths;
- network adaptation without a central planner.

Good for route finding, controller ecology, branching musical form, and distributed visual growth.

### Basal cognition / bioelectric morphogenesis
**Grounding:** active scientific research with varying strength across claims. Treat broad claims about cognition in cells/tissues cautiously; use concrete signaling and morphogenetic relations when possible.

Useful operations:

- local signaling changes global morphology;
- target-state maintenance;
- error correction in form;
- competing regional goals.

### Xenobiology / alternative biochemical alphabets
The source palette discusses XNA, noncanonical amino acids, alternate information chemistry, and synthetic cells.

Creative use: change the **alphabet, compatibility rules, replication constraints, or binding grammar** underlying a system. Do not reduce this to “alien slime.”

Canonical relatives: Primitive Replacement, Taxonomic Contagion, Substrate Transference.

### Symbiosis / endosymbiosis
Useful when components should retain partial autonomy while becoming mutually necessary.

Operational possibilities:

- one subsystem becomes another’s organelle;
- separate jurisdictions become metabolically coupled;
- outputs of A become required inputs of B;
- identity emerges from a maintained partnership rather than a single owner.

### Fungal / mycelial networks
Use for branching resource exchange, distributed sensing, rerouting, decomposition, and heterogeneous connectivity. Avoid treating every branching visual as “mycelial.”

### Autopoiesis / enaction
Useful when a system’s boundary and identity are maintained through ongoing activity rather than assumed in advance.

Operational questions:

- What processes regenerate the boundary?
- What must remain coupled for the system to count as the same organism?
- Which perturbations break identity vs reorganize it?

Strong fit for stateful controllers and video identity-through-time experiments.

### Dissipative structures / nonequilibrium thermodynamics
Use when order should exist only because energy/material is continuously flowing through the system.

Operational descendants:

- threshold-driven phase changes;
- decay without replenishment;
- structures that vanish when throughput stops;
- competing rates and energy budgets.

### Complex systems / attractors / bifurcation
Use carefully. “Attractor,” “chaos,” and “bifurcation” are easy decorative words.

Make them operational through measurable recurrence, parameter sweeps, regime changes, hysteresis, and perturbation recovery.

Canonical destinations: SRE/TOPOS, experimental method, route/path systems.

### Quantum-biology and other contested material
The source palette intentionally includes speculative and contested scientific areas because they can generate unusual relations.

**Rule:** preserve their status. A contested hypothesis can be a brilliant art lens without being presented as settled science. Translate the proposed relation into a creative operator, then separate that operator from the scientific truth claim.

## Invented personas and fictional disciplines

Both palettes include invented thinkers/scientists. Their status is **FLAVOR / GENERATIVE SCAFFOLD** unless their proposed operation is independently specified.

Invented entities are useful for:

- combining several real lenses into a temporary research voice;
- naming a weird but repeatable analytic job;
- creating a brainstorming panel with deliberately different blind spots.

They are not sources of evidence. Extract the operation and route it into the operator registry or idea garden.

## Crossbreeding lenses

Do not stack five proper nouns into one prompt. Crossbreed by mechanism.

Example:

`Umwelt → define a detector`  
`reaction–diffusion → define local spread/inhibition`  
`apparatus theory → make the camera respond to the same field`

That can become one coherent system: a detector senses an abstract variable, the sensed variable spreads/inhibits locally across anatomy, and the recording medium degrades according to the same field.

The proper names can disappear; the causal machinery remains.

## Epistemic rule

The source palettes intentionally mix established science, active research, contested hypotheses, philosophy, art theory, fiction, and invented personas. Keep that mixture creatively. Do **not** flatten it epistemically.

For factual scientific work, verify externally. For AI SLOP ideation, use the palettes as **relation generators**, then label the resulting mechanism at the correct status.
