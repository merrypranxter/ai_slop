## Current architecture

The Little Guy Machine now treats each cognitive operator as more than a named prompt gimmick. Every mind has:
- a cognitive family
- a chaos rating from 1–5
- compatibility tags
- friction tags
- recommended pairings
- a role hint describing how it should behave inside a stack

The generator receives this metadata and is instructed to preserve distinct jobs between minds. Low-chaos minds stabilize, measure, narrate, or regulate. High-chaos minds are expected to create genuine structural discontinuity rather than merely adding more "weirdness."

Smart stack generation also uses this metadata. Random stack generation has been replaced with chemistry-aware selection that favors:
- different cognitive families
- different jurisdictions
- productive pairing relationships
- useful friction
- appropriate chaos balance

The FUCK ME UP mode deliberately favors high-chaos cross-family and cross-jurisdiction stacks.

Starred generations softly influence later stack construction. Minds that appear in starred runs receive a limited preference boost, so taste bends future probability without turning the system into a clone machine.

## Phase 2 — Ten new Temporary Minds

### SCALE WITCH — Cross-Scale Betrayal
**Jurisdiction:** scale transitions  
Force the system to be examined at at least three distinct scales: micro, meso, and macro. Do not allow explanations valid at one scale to remain automatically valid at the others. Identify a property that reverses, dissolves, or becomes paradoxical when scale changes, and make the work depend on that betrayal.

### HYSTERESIS HAG — Path-Dependent State Memory
**Jurisdiction:** path dependence  
Do not let the present state depend only on current conditions. Require the system to remember the route by which it arrived. If two states look identical now but emerged through different histories, they must behave differently going forward. The scar of the path is part of the mechanism.

### BUDGET GHOUL — Conservation Pressure
**Jurisdiction:** conservation laws  
Assume one important quantity cannot be created from nothing and cannot disappear without consequence. Define what is conserved: energy, attention, contradiction, tenderness, noise, complexity, or another explicit quantity. Every gain must be paid for by a loss, displacement, storage, leak, or transfer somewhere else.

### HECKLER PRIEST — Adversarial Interpretation
**Jurisdiction:** adversarial interpretation  
Generate as if a hostile but intelligent interpreter is waiting to misread, flatten, or exploit the obvious reading. Do not merely become obscure. Anticipate the strongest bad-faith interpretation, then redesign the structure so that hostile reading either collapses, becomes less interesting than the intended reading, or accidentally proves the deeper point.

### PHASE BANSHEE — Threshold Catastrophe
**Jurisdiction:** phase transitions  
Treat the system as gradually accumulating pressure toward a qualitative jump. Quantitative change alone is not enough. Identify the control parameter, the hidden accumulation, the threshold, and the new regime that appears after crossing it. The post-threshold state must obey a different rule set rather than simply becoming more intense.

### AGENCY THIEF — Role Redistribution
**Jurisdiction:** agency distribution  
Audit who or what is normally allowed to cause change, decide, remember, authorize, or carry intention. Remove one of those powers from its default owner and reassign it elsewhere: to a byproduct, a tool, a crowd, a silence, a wound, a delay, a container, or a side effect. Recalculate the world under the new agency map.

### CONSTRAINT DRUID — Constraint Ecology
**Jurisdiction:** constraint ecology  
Do not treat constraints as isolated limits. Model them as an ecosystem that competes, feeds, suppresses, amplifies, and shelters one another. Tightening one constraint may loosen another. Removing one may cause invasive overgrowth elsewhere. Generate by changing the ecology of limitations rather than solving constraints one by one.

### IF GHOST — Counterfactual Load Bearing
**Jurisdiction:** counterfactual dependency  
Identify a nearby unrealized alternative that the current reality depends on for its meaning, stability, or coherence. Do not simply imagine a different world. Show how the actual system is propped up, haunted, or organized by the roads not taken. The absent branch must exert real pressure on the present branch.

### SYMMETRY SHIV — Necessary Asymmetry
**Jurisdiction:** symmetry breaking  
Begin from a state where multiple possibilities are equally valid, equally beautiful, or equally likely. Then force a tiny asymmetry to get amplified until one branch dominates. Make the break explicit: what tiny bias got selected, why it propagated, and what world exists only because symmetry could not remain intact.

### JURISDICTION EATER — Self-Mutating Rule Domain
**Jurisdiction:** self-modifying jurisdiction  
A thinking rule may not keep the same jurisdiction for the entire run. It must begin governing one domain, then under explicit pressure migrate into a neighboring domain and alter itself in transit. Track what changed, what remained invariant, and what new operations became possible only because the jurisdiction itself drifted.

## Stack chemistry labels

The app calculates:
- average chaos
- number of distinct cognitive families
- number of distinct jurisdictions
- average pairing strength

Stacks are displayed as:
- controlled
- volatile
- feral
- critical

The purpose is not to reward maximum chaos. The goal is productive cognitive friction: enough disagreement to generate genuinely new structure while preserving distinct operational responsibilities.

## Phase 2 design principle

The important change is that minds are no longer just prompt fragments placed next to each other.

They are becoming a small synthetic ecology of cognitive operators.

The system now tracks:
- what kind of thinking each mind performs
- how violently it changes structure
- what kinds of minds complement it
- what kinds of assumptions it attacks
- whether it should stabilize or destabilize a stack
- which cognitive mechanisms the user repeatedly likes

This gives future work a foundation for rule breeding, lineage selection, adaptive stack composition, and eventually user-specific cognitive ecology without collapsing into repetitive recommendation behavior.
