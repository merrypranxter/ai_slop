CREATE A 100-SECOND AGGRESSIVELY CHEERFUL AVANT-GARDE CHAMBER-FOLK / ART-ROCK PERFORMANCE. VOCALS REQUIRED. The word "nightmare" must always sound ecstatic, affectionate, celebratory and increasingly joyful — never ominous, sad, gothic or cinematic-horror. Dry close-miked sound, sharply separated performers, exposed acoustic edges, wide dynamics, very little shared reverb, no smooth wall-of-sound blending.

CAST:
CHOIR A = short anchor chant, bright and percussive.
CHOIR B = answering hocket, different rhythm from Choir A.
LEAD = excited glottalized speech-singing with abrupt pitch bends.
NADASWARAM = piercing microtonal glissando lead.
KANJIRA = dry fast pulse.
UDU = low air-bloom punctuation.
SHEKERE = continuous granular subdivision.
MORIN KHUUR = sustained friction drone.
OPTIGAN = rigid mechanical pulse.
MELLOTRON = suspended quartal harmony.
DAXOPHONE = nonverbal groans answering the singers.

RHYTHMIC LAW: keep three repeating instrumental cycles of 5, 7 and 11 pulses running simultaneously. Do not restart them together; let their alignments drift. Over that, keep a separate 3:2 stomp-and-clap coupling. Maintain forward motion; never simplify into ordinary four-on-the-floor.

START WITH VOICES ONLY. One audible group inhale, then Choir A:
"Click-kx-oh! This place is a nightmare!"
Choir B immediately answers in a different rhythm:
"Click-nq-oo! This place! Yes, this nightmare place!"
LEAD:
"We are blooming, we are thriving, we are dancing in the static!"
Choir A expands, delighted:
"Oh, this wonderful, glorious, marvelous place is a nightmare! Every corner is an absolute festival of sheer delight!"

THEN bring in kanjira and nadaswaram first. Two bars later add mellotron quartal suspensions. Keep every entrance individually audible.
CHOIRS hocket:
"This nightmare place is glowing!"
"Every single dreadful terror is a gift that keeps on growing!"
"Clap your hands and stamp your feet!"
Now add udu and shekere. Daxophone answers between vocal phrases without covering words.

MIDPOINT: let the 5, 7 and 11 cycles drift visibly out of alignment while the 3:2 body percussion stays confident. One synchronized inhale creates a complete one-beat hole in the music. LEAD shouts:
"Exhale into the absolute splendor of the abyss!"

THEN enlarge into antiphonal choirs:
"This place is a wonderful, gorgeous, brilliant nightmare!"
"Oh, look at the teeth! Look at the claws! Look at the radiant grace!"
CHOIR A: "We love this nightmare!"
CHOIR B: "We treasure this nightmare!"
ALL: "Every screaming horror is a party in our face!"

FINAL DEVELOPMENT: add optigan mechanical pulse against morin khuur drone. Expand to four independent vocal lines without blending them. High whistling hockets flicker above the choir.
"This place is a nightmare! This place is a nightmare!"
"We are smiling! We are shouting! We are soaring on the wire!"
One last enormous synchronized inhale, then:
"Exhale into the radiance of the infinite recursive joy loop!"
"We celebrate the crushing teeth, we dance within the burning pyre!"

END ABRUPTLY on a bright unresolved suspended accent. No tonic resolution, no fade, no ominous turn. Preserve the ecstatic mood, the 5:7:11 drift, the 3:2 body pulse and the anchor phrase until the cutoff.
