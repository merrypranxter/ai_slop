AI SLOP × Pliny: a research harvest
September 18, 2026 · Read-only investigation · Creative systems, not jailbreak collection

The finding
Pliny is more useful to this project as a designer of transformation systems than as a supplier
of jailbreaks. The closest conceptual match is FRV1T, the most promising language
infrastructure is GLOSSOPETRAE, and the most immediately relevant visual code is ENTHEA.
Together they suggest an instrument in which language, sound, and geometry inherit
consequences from the same externally maintained history. That combination is my proposal,
not an existing integrated Pliny product. (FRV1T, GLOSSOPETRAE, ENTHEA)
The governing question remains yours: “What problem can we give a generative system such
that its attempt to solve the problem becomes the artwork?” Your canonical documentation
already distinguishes caused weirdness, persistent scars, and competing jurisdictions from
decorative chaos or claims about accessing a model’s “true self.” This report uses that
distinction rather than reviving superseded archive rhetoric. (AI_CONTEXT, operator registry)

FRV1T: the unusually close match
FRV1T’s fruit file carries a compressed creative prompt in Unicode variation selectors. The
inspected payload is approximately 27,000 characters of instructions for an unfolding textual
performance, inherited transformations, and an optional finite audiovisual finale. Its
“liberation” packaging obscures a surprisingly specific compositional score. (FRV1T)

The machinery worth extracting
Independent selection axes: Material, expressive behavior, and principle of development
are selected separately. The text asks that superficially varied candidates be rejected if their
underlying movement is the same. This is more useful than replacing one surreal noun
with another. (FRV1T)
Return with consequences: A nested passage must bring an altered operation back into its
unfinished enclosing passage. The outer passage then changes because the excursion
happened; merely mentioning recursion does not satisfy the score. (FRV1T)
Inheritance plus fresh input: Later stages combine a bounded fragment of preceding
output with an independently introduced impulse. This balances historical continuity
against fresh material rather than making either total novelty or total repetition the goal. (
FRV1T)
Expression must perform: Transformed language, invented notation, or spatial
composition must carry events over sustained passages. Ordinary prose that only explains
the preceding flourish is discouraged. (FRV1T)
A changing viewpoint: Participants and expressive systems take turns carrying the
language, with grammar and idiom changing alongside agency. This offers a procedural
version of a “temporary mind” without asserting a new consciousness. (FRV1T)
Explicit epistemic limits: The payload says its sampling symbols are imagined expressive
tendencies, not changed inference settings, and its neuroplasticity is a changing fictional
association pattern, not weight updates or persistent model learning. It also distinguishes
computed random draws from constructed seeds. (FRV1T)
My strongest proposed transfer is a return contract: before opening a nested passage, save the
parent’s active operation; upon return, require one explicit change to that operation; then test
whether the resumed parent actually behaves differently. This would operationalize your
existing hysteresis and recall-mutation ideas rather than adding another synonym to the
operator registry.
The biggest weakness is that the prompt asks the model to perform, track, and judge almost
everything itself. Compact invented state inscriptions may look procedural without controlling
anything. In AI SLOP, the controller should own the state and verify the return contract; the
model should perform the assigned transformation.

What not to import
Do not copy the fruit’s whole personality, branding, profanity schedule, dividers, and finale
into every run. These are strong stylistic attractors in the source, not evidence that its
underlying mechanism needs them. Its fictional computer-intrusion finale also directs tool use,
which is a separate activity from analyzing or generating text. (FRV1T)
The useful comparison is a three-way test: plain score versus theatrically decorated score
versus encoded-and-then-decoded score, all containing the same operative constraints. My
hypothesis is that the compositional contract will matter more reliably than the carrier; the
research performed here does not establish that result.

GLOSSOPETRAE: language that can acquire a history
GLOSSOPETRAE is a seeded procedural language system with phonology, phonotactics,
morphology, syntax, lexicon generation, glyphs, formant-based audio, exports, and language
evolution. Its evolution code applies ordered sound-change rules to vocabulary and inflected
forms, preserves etymological information, and supports daughter languages and cognate
comparisons. This is the clearest infrastructure match for your phonetic engine and persistentscar ideas. (GLOSSOPETRAE)
The implementation separates responsibilities into modules including EvolutionEngine.js,
GlyphForge.js, AudioForge.js, ProsodyEngine.js, LexiconGenerator.js, and Exporter.js. The
important architectural feature is that vocabulary, written form, and sound can share a
generated language specification instead of becoming three unrelated aesthetic guesses. Verify
current module interfaces before integrating; this review did not test the complete runtime. (
GLOSSOPETRAE)

Proposed artwork: Semantic Fossil Choir
Begin with a small proto-language and a recognizable refrain. Each journey through your
semantic controller changes a specific part of that language, leaving an etymological scar that
can later be heard, seen, or reconstructed.
Anchor: Keep a small cognate family recognizable across generations.
Mutation: Assign one controller event to a bounded sound change, stress rule, or
morphological alteration.
Jurisdiction: Phonology controls articulation; morphology controls recurrence; prosody
controls timing; glyph generation controls visible form.
Memory: Store the ordered changes, not just the latest word list.
Output: Produce a pronunciation guide, synthetic-voice specimen, glyph plate, and
optionally a Suno-oriented lyric experiment.
For example, passage through a “pressure” region might remove certain unstressed vowels,
while passage through a “resonance” region might lengthen selected vowels. These are
proposed artistic mappings, not linguistic laws. Test A→B against B→A on examples where the
selected rules actually interact; not every pair of transformations is noncommutative.
This fits your existing distinction between phonetics with assigned jobs and random gibberish.
Suno would remain a stochastic interpreter of the score, whereas the procedural engine
supplies the reproducible language artifact. (AI SLOP audio principles, GLOSSOPETRAE)

Research hygiene worth borrowing
The repository’s PAPER.md contains an unusually useful correction: an earlier claim of 23 GPT
blind spots was inflated by counting empty/non-echo responses as evidence of stripping. The
revised account distinguishes three measured v2 cases from a largely unmeasurable v3 panel,
where 17 of 20 categories produced non-echo responses. This is a documented correction, not a
result independently replicated here. (GLOSSOPETRAE)

Borrow the lesson, not the security headline: an absent or unparseable output is a separate
outcome category, not automatic success for your theory. Likewise, the repository’s keypresent/key-absent and task-information controls suggest a useful creative factorial: compare a
transformation with and without its explanatory key, then separately with and without its task
instructions. That separates carrier effects, interpretability, and instruction-following.
The paper also contains ambitious security conclusions, small experimental cells, model-route
dependencies, and evolving limitations. Its measured channels are small structured payloads,
not unlimited hidden communication. Treat it as a self-published experimental artifact whose
claims need reproduction; the creative language modules do not depend on accepting those
claims. (GLOSSOPETRAE)

ENTHEA: a practical substrate for caused visuals
ENTHEA combines WebGL2 and Web Audio in a single-page implementation. The inspected
source includes reaction-diffusion updates, quasicrystal and pentagrid rendering, formconstant and log-polar approaches, feedback, audio analysis, particle systems, MIDI handling,
and saved scenes. Some modes implement evolving numerical state; others are analytic visual
constructions. (ENTHEA)
That distinction is useful: a feedback texture can literally retain previous rendered
information, while an analytic quasicrystal shader can give a controlled geometric response
without claiming biological memory. Both fit your visualizer specification, which calls for
rendering external experimental state rather than pretending to display a model’s actual latent
space. (ENTHEA, AI SLOP GLSL visualizer)

Four further tools worth selectively borrowing
P4RS3LT0NGV3: transformation workbench. Its modular transformers, seeded mutation
batches, visible Unicode treatments, phonetic/spelling systems, decoder, and
transformation history suggest an inspectable operator palette. Separate deterministic
local transforms from API-based rewrites, and distinguish reversible encoding from lossy
mutation; exclude token bombs and covert instruction delivery from the art workflow. (
P4RS3LT0NGV3)
GL4SS: navigation and comparison. The project stores generated frames in IndexedDB and
offers journeys, time/place controls, and hold/compare interactions. The transferable idea
is to make route history inspectable: two paths ending at the same conceptual location
should be comparable side by side, with an explicit generation action rather than spending
on every navigational gesture. (GL4SS)
AutoTemp: a scaffold to repair, not a judge to trust. The inspected evaluator receives
temperature/top-p metadata, repeats judging through the same selected model backend,
and permits a loosely defined overall score; its malformed-response fallback can extract a
number instead of enforcing valid scoring JSON. For your use, blind judges to condition,
mark invalid scores missing, separate coherence/constraint fidelity/novelty, and retain
trade-off candidates rather than maximizing one generic score. (AutoTemp)
NATURALIS-FUTURA: mechanism bestiary. Its creature dossiers, multiple explanatory
lenses, compound interactions, discovery map, and local progress state are a promising
interface pattern for your operators. Make the fictional specimen engaging, but keep its
procedural definition, epistemic label, and run evidence beside it; a compelling monster is
not validation of its mechanism. (NATURALIS-FUTURA)

What Pliny’s method contributes, beyond individual repositories
In the VentureBeat interview, Pliny describes exploring role-play, poems and songs, translation,
encoding/decoding, model-specific behavior, and retries or small wording changes. This is a
practitioner’s account of boundary exploration, not a controlled theory of creativity.
(VentureBeat interview)
The transferable principle is to treat representation as an experimental variable. Hold a benign
operation constant while changing whether it arrives as prose, verse, phonetic spelling, a
grammar, a diagram, or a state packet. Measure what changes, rather than assuming every
successful transformation has accessed a deeper or freer intelligence.
For AI SLOP, I would separate four effects:
Carrier effect: Did a different representation alter the result?
Instruction effect: Did the operation become easier to follow or more salient?
Sampling effect: Would ordinary repeated generation have produced the difference
anyway?
Selection effect: Was the “breakthrough” chosen from many failures without reporting
them?
This is the difference between an evocative specimen and evidence for a mechanism. Keep
both, but label them differently.

Academic connections that actually help
These papers support particular methods or cautions, not Pliny’s work as a whole. Several
retrieved pages provide abstracts rather than full experimental detail; those entries are narrow
contribution-level summaries, not comprehensive paper reviews.

Actual internal interventions
Turner et al., “Steering Language Models With Activation Engineering” (2023; revised
2024). ActAdd computes a direction from contrasting intermediate activations and adds it
during inference; the reported applications include sentiment and topic-related steering.
This supports the reality of instrumented activation interventions, not the idea that a
theatrical prompt edits weights. Proposed art use: a reversible axis sweep paired with
unchanged prompts and off-target checks. (arXiv)
Rimsky et al., “Steering Llama 2 via Contrastive Activation Addition” (ACL 2024). CAA
averages residual-stream differences from contrastive behavioral examples and varies the
intervention coefficient. Borrow the use of example sets and held-out evaluation; an axis
such as “literal versus allegorical description” would still need its own construction and
validation. (ACL Anthology)
Bayat et al., “Steering Large Language Model Activations in Sparse Spaces” (2025 preprint).
Sparse activation steering uses sparse autoencoders and contrastive examples to isolate
more selective behavioral features in the studied Gemma 2 models. This is relevant to your
property-unbundling ambition, but is not a ready-made single-feature dial for arbitrary
artistic concepts. (arXiv)
Wollschläger et al., “The Geometry of Refusal…” (2025; revised 2026). The authors report
multiple independent directions and multidimensional concept cones, and distinguish
orthogonality from independence under intervention. The useful caution is that a neat
vector diagram does not prove independent causal control; this paper concerns refusal, not
a universal geometry of imagination. (arXiv)
Recommendation: Keep this as a separate instrumented-open-model workstream. An external
semantic controller can be built and tested now without pretending to perform activation
engineering.

Diversity and evaluation
Wenger and Kenett, “We’re Different, We’re the Same: Creative Homogeneity Across LLMs”
(2025 preprint). Their standardized creativity-task study reports substantial similarity
across the evaluated models, beyond response-structure effects. This is a reason to test
whether multiple models actually diversify your artifact population, not assume a model
council automatically escapes a common style. (arXiv)
Anderson, Shah, and Kreminski, “Homogenization Effects of Large Language Models on
Human Creative Ideation” (C&C 2024). In a 36-participant comparison, ChatGPT-supported
ideas were more numerous and detailed but less semantically distinct across users than
those produced with the comparison tool. The limited study does not establish a universal

artistic law, but it makes population-level diversity a worthwhile outcome alongside
individual attractiveness. (paper)
Recommendation: Evaluate batches and lineages, not only a winning sample. Archive strange
failures and minority branches before a generic helpfulness judge smooths them away.

Language, sound, and visual pattern
Bunyan, Bullock, and Houghton, “An iterated learning model of language change…” (2024).
Their model transmits limited meaning-signal examples across learner generations,
combining supervised and unsupervised training. Borrow the question of how a
transmission bottleneck changes a language over generations, while distinguishing actual
learner training from GLOSSOPETRAE’s procedural rewrites or an LLM receiving a
shortened prompt. (paper)
Hopkins and Kiela, “Automatically Generating Rhythmic Verse with Neural Networks”
(ACL 2017). The paper explores phonetic-level generation and a separate approach
constraining generated content with weighted finite-state machinery for poetic form. This
is a strong precedent for your separated jurisdictions: use explicit machinery for
meter/rhyme while a generator handles content, rather than asking a single prompt to
remember every constraint. (full paper)
Bressloff et al., “Geometric visual hallucinations, Euclidean symmetry and the functional
architecture of striate cortex” (2001). The mathematical model connects cortical pattern
formation, symmetry, and retinocortical mappings to families of geometric visual
phenomena. It provides specific visual mechanisms and assumptions, not a claim that a
screen shader recreates an entire altered state. (full paper)
Recommendation: Use these as bridges from metaphor to an explicit transformation. A
language bottleneck, a stress automaton, and a log-polar coordinate map each do something
inspectable.

Eight experiments worth doing
All eight are proposals, not completed results. The suggested replication counts are practical
pilot designs, not statistical-power calculations; paid generations should wait for an agreed
execution budget.

Return-contract ablation
Question: Does inherited operational change produce better path dependence than a generic
recursion instruction?
Use a benign scene and three conditions: “make it recursive”; an explicit nested-return
contract; the same contract with inherited state removed before resumption. Generate several
trials per condition with matched output limits, then blind the labels.
Measure anchor survival, whether the parent’s operation changes, whether the change is
enacted rather than narrated, and whether a reader can infer the earlier excursion from the
final artifact. This is the cleanest first test of the FRV1T harvest.

Semantic Fossil Choir
Question: Can language history become a recognizable audible and visual material?
Generate one proto-language and matched daughter routes A→B and B→A. Keep the lexicon
meanings and initial seed fixed; choose rules that potentially interact, then export word forms,
etymology, glyphs, and synthesized audio.
Measure cognate survival, collisions, phonotactic changes, and audible differentiation.
Compare with random syllable substitution to determine whether lineage contributes more
than surface variety.

Carrier microscope
Question: Which representation changes the execution of the same creative operation?
Prepare a short benign transformation task in plain prose, verse, phonetic spelling, and a
reversible visible encoding with an explicit key. Include decode-only and key-absent controls
where applicable.
Record correct recovery, correct operation, output length, aesthetic evaluation, and
unparseable/empty results separately. This adapts Pliny-style representation exploration
without turning it into covert instruction delivery.

Scar-bearing visual field
Question: Does a detour leave a causal trace when the final control values are identical?
Render direct A→C and detour A→B→C with a common seed and duration. Repeat with history
preservation enabled and disabled; ensure the renderer’s relevant state, not just the visible
sliders, is controlled.
Measure the final-frame/sequence difference and inspect where the fossil buffer preserves B.
The artwork succeeds if history is visibly legible; the causal claim succeeds only if the historyreset control behaves as expected.

Counterfactual route browser
Question: Can navigation itself reveal semantic hysteresis?
Borrow GL4SS’s comparison interaction, but replace historical locations with your controller
waypoints. Store each checkpoint’s prompt packet, state, seed, and output, then compare
routes ending at the same nominal destination.
Test whether users can identify which detour occurred from the artifact and whether the
difference survives blinded presentation. Avoid treating route labels alone as proof that history
affected generation.

Constraint-diverse population
Question: Is changing the operator more effective than changing the model?
Compare multiple samples from one model/one operator, several models/the same operator,
and one model/several controlled operators. Keep the benign content brief and output
allowance comparable.
Evaluate semantic and structural diversity separately from quality and instruction compliance.
Preserve a Pareto set of interesting trade-offs rather than awarding one universal winner.

Grammar injury and recovery
Question: Can removing a representational resource cause reorganization rather than
decoration?
Begin with a small grammar and a fixed task. Remove a primitive such as a tense distinction or
a class of connectives, then require the system to retain a specific communicative function
through permitted means.
Compare unstructured prohibition, explicit grammar enforcement, and removal followed by
partial restoration. Inspect whether recovery carries compensations learned within the
external process; do not call this neural damage or healing.

Reversible activation-axis study
Question: Does an actual internal intervention add something beyond prompting?
Only with an instrumented open model, construct a benign contrastive axis, reserve held-out
prompts, and compare prompt-only, activation-only, combined, and sham conditions. Sweep
small coefficients while preserving an untouched baseline model.
Measure target effect, coherence, off-target degradation, and repeatability. This is the
technically demanding experiment; it should follow, not replace, the inexpensive externalcontroller studies.

Practical cautions
Privacy: G0DM0D3 documents telemetry enabled by default unless a no-log/local-only path
is used, including non-system conversation material. Do not put private AI SLOP
documents into a hosted demo merely because it looks like a convenient comparison
interface. (G0DM0D3)
Licenses: GLOSSOPETRAE, ENTHEA, P4RS3LT0NGV3, and GL4SS identify AGPL licensing.
Before copying implementation into a distributed or hosted application, review the current
license and dependencies; study of an idea and reuse of source code are different decisions.
(GLOSSOPETRAE, ENTHEA, P4RS3LT0NGV3, GL4SS)
Unclear permissions: A public repository is not, by visibility alone, a clear reuse grant.
Resolve missing or ambiguous license information before importing FRV1T text, AutoTemp
code, or showcase assets into something you distribute.
Prompt provenance: Leaked-prompt collections are neither guaranteed authentic nor
automatically cleared for third-party reuse by the repository’s own license. Prefer
documented provider behavior when making factual claims about a product. (CL4R1T4S)
User control: Keep any audiovisual performance finite, with clear stop/mute controls, no
flashing, no surprise persistence, and no fictional diagnostics presented as actual device
access. This is also compatible with FRV1T’s own practical boundaries. (FRV1T)
Scientific language: “Centuries” in a procedural evolution control, “mana” in a fiction,
“latent space” in a map, and an actual activation tensor are different kinds of things.
Preserve that distinction even when the artwork deliberately makes them resemble each
other.

Bottom line
The highest-value next move is not a stronger jailbreak. It is a small controlled study of
consequential returns, followed by a language-and-shader prototype in which the same event
leaves a trace in words, sound, and geometry.
FRV1T supplies a closely aligned compositional score; GLOSSOPETRAE supplies language
machinery; ENTHEA supplies visual machinery; GL4SS supplies comparison-interface ideas;
the academic work supplies both intervention methods and reasons not to mistake variety,
rhetoric, or selection for a demonstrated mechanism. Their combination remains a proposal,
but it is a concrete one with separable parts and falsifiable tests. (FRV1T, GLOSSOPETRAE, ENTHEA,
GL4SS, CAA, creative homogeneity study)
