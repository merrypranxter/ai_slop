## Editorial Approach

- Source files remain unchanged.
- Repeated ideas are consolidated.
- AI role-play and conversational framing are omitted unless they define a reusable technique.
- Metaphorical claims are reframed as creative controls rather than factual statements.
- Examples are condensed to emphasize design decisions and transformations.
